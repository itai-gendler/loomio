# Loomio

Loomio gives groups a dedicated place to share context, discuss proposals, gather input, vote and record clear outcomes, across time zones and without meetings. Groups and their subgroups hold discussions; a discussion's thread gathers comments, proposals and polls of several kinds, each person's vote and reason, and the outcome that states what was decided. Group settings decide who can find a group, how people join and what ordinary members may do beside its admins.

## Intent

Loomio is open-source software for making decisions together, used by cooperatives, nonprofits, boards, unions, community groups, membership organizations and distributed teams. It moves important decisions out of scattered emails and chat threads, involves the right people, helps work through differences and agree on what happens next. Every discussion, reason and outcome stays together, creating a searchable record of what was decided and why, so decisions stay transparent, inclusive and easy to revisit.
